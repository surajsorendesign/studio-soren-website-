01 The Challenge

Not dashboards. Decisions.

Supply chains generate more signals than any team can act on, and most tools stop at showing them. Optaro is built to act. The name comes from the Latin optāre, to choose or strive for the best outcome, and that is the work its AI agents do every day.

Optaro senses what is changing, analyses what it means and executes the decision, with a human taking the final call. That loop of sense, analyse, execute became the spine of the brand, carried through a messaging hierarchy every deliverable had to align with.

Visual direction

The human is in control. Always.

Most AI brands put the machine on stage: glowing orbs, neural meshes, a product that seems to act alone. Optaro is data source agnostic. It takes whatever a client already has and turns it into a clear call a person can approve, adjust or decline.

The moodboard set that tone. Sharp geometric forms and structured grids stand for the quiet, reliable system underneath, and bold contrast and human portraits sit on top, so a decision always reads as someone’s choice.

Moodboard: geometric minimalism. Sharp forms, bold contrasts, structured grid systems.

02 Logo & Brand Guidelines

The multi-level brand we crafted

Customers would see this brand for four seconds on a shared screen before moving to the next deck, so the voice had to be crisp, confident, value led and human. Sophisticated, not complicated.

The visual system matches. One typeface, a ten shade purple scale, and a mark that can sit next to a P&L slide without looking decorative. It all ships as a single guideline document the team can work from.
